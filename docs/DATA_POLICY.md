# Data distribution policy for the GitHub candidate

The original Santander SIoT files and their graph-derived topology have not been included in this **public-upload candidate**. Provenance, source and licensing conditions must be verified before redistribution. The exported `E3_36_SCENARIO_104_SUPPORT_v2.zip` contains generated observations and topology-derived records and is retained separately for controlled research audit; do not upload it wholesale to public GitHub/Zenodo until its redistribution status is resolved.

Checkpoints and scalers are included as author-generated research artifacts; before making the repository public, confirm coauthor authorization and any upstream model/code licensing restrictions. No explicit open-source license has been granted yet.

Paper claims of a fully reproducible end-to-end pipeline must await a fresh-environment execution check. Result-summary verification from the included CSVs is supported now.
